# Bonus Attack Terminology

In this project, "보너스 어택" means the former auto line clear damage system: when same-type rows/columns are automatically cleared after a valid expression, their accumulated damage is shown in the BonusAttackSumValue panel and applied to the enemy. Relevant implementation remains named autoLineClearDamage* in BattleSceneController for backward compatibility, but user-facing wording should use 보너스 어택.
